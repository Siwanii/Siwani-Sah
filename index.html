About

About Me

My passion for technology began during my undergraduate studies at Visvesvaraya Technological University in Bangalore, India, where I earned my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science. It was there that I first discovered my love for building software solutions that make a real impact.

After completing my bachelor's degree, I joined Professional Computer System (P) Ltd as a Software Engineer, where I had the opportunity to work on large-scale enterprise ERP systems. During my time there, I contributed to critical projects for organizations like the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nepal (ICAN), Nepal Tourism Board (NTB), and Nepal Election Commission. These experiences taught me the importance of writing clean, maintainable code and working collaboratively with clients to deliver solutions that exceed expectations.

Driven by a desire to deepen my knowledge and explore cutting-edge technologies, I decided to pursue my Master's degree at Florida Polytechnic University. As a Graduate Assistant, I've had the privilege of working closely with professors on research initiatives in AI/ML, distributed systems, and cloud computing. I also serve as a Teaching Assistant for Machine Learning and C Programming courses, where I help guide the next generation of computer Engineers.

As a lifelong learner, I'm continuously expanding my expertise and am currently pursuing certification in Prompt Design with Vertex AI to stay at the forefront of AI-driven solutions. When I'm not coding or researching, I'm passionate about sharing knowledge, mentoring peers, and contributing to the tech community through my role as Technical Lead at GDG.

My goal is to continue growing as a software engineer, contributing to innovative projects that solve real-world problems, and inspiring others in the tech community through my work.
